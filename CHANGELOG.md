## Unreleased

First release. StreamAssistant2 runs alongside the lotsofs Twitch stream:

- It reads chat, answers its FAQ commands, and takes admin commands.
- It announces subs, gift bombs, cheers, StreamElements tips and raids with sounds, speech and chat lines.
- It handles the channel-point rewards.
- It sets up OBS for the game being played: capture sources, background, borders, the layout's colours, and a left panel that rotates through LiveSplit's splits.
- Its own window shows the connections, the log, and what it has set in OBS.
